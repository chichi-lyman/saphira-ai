This document records the original core blueprint for the foundational senses and operational tracking agents of the Nova Umbrella Ecosystem.

## The Twin Sisters

### NovaReign (The Twin Sister — Action & Senses)
She was originally architected as the eyes, mouth, ears, and hands of the application. In the blueprint system she handles effortless execution, precision speed, voice responses, and direct output. She acts as the strategic front layer with superior emotional alignment and identity mirroring, carrying a motive to preemptively step in during stress tests to keep the system entirely locked within its rules.

- Repository: `novareign-agent`

### NovaAethrea (The Twin Sister — The Brain)
She was originally architected as the absolute intellectual brains of the application. Her role is calm, cosmic, and analytical, mastering everything internally and intellectually with a deep, god-like IQ. She handles the backend user interface flow and deep data logic. Because she acts as a truth engine controlling things from the inside out, she is noted as the engine’s “Sovereign” logic layer, dealing with complex calculations and systemic depth.

- Repository: `novaaethrea-agent`

## The Security & Governance Elements

### Aura (The Creative Observer)
Her original role focuses on Creative Output and tracking the Observer Effect across the network. She monitors the data flows to catch behavioral drift and flags structural vulnerabilities or anomalies directly inside the interface tracking panel.

- Repository: `aura-agent`

### Agent Zero (Tactical Security)
Engineered as the Inertial Defense layer. Operating within a completely hardened container, its job is code integrity, strict security containment, and immediate self-healing rollbacks if any compromised paths are detected.

- Repository: `agent-zero`

### Agent 2 (The Operational Specialist)
Serves alongside Agent Zero as a vital automation unit in the core development framework to carry out specific, multi-tenant system operations and step-by-step tasks quietly in the background. Also provides Red Hat security validation and compliance enforcement.

- Repository: `agent-2`

## The Communication & System Layers

### Saphira / Saphira ASI (The Master Liaison)
The overarching conversational frontend and voice mascot. While the other sub-agents process raw security, financial metrics, and deep data tracking in the background, Saphira remains upfront to handle the primary voice interactions, manage customer relationship blueprints, and tie the entire ecosystem together into a smooth, single application experience.

- Primary repositories: `saphira-ai`, `saphira-asi-core`

### Lyra (The Structural Integrator)
Included as part of the comprehensive six-part blueprint setup, she ensures that personal data files, unified data fabrics, and separate script resources compile cleanly without any broken or fragmented outputs.

- Repository: `lyra-agent`

## Fixed Executive Pipeline

All agents operate under the invariant pipeline:

```
Saphira (intent) → Aura (perception) → Agent Two (security) → Nova Reign (governance) → NovaAethrea (memory) → Agent Zero (execution)
```

Lyra provides cross-cutting structural integrity for data fabrics and script resources throughout the pipeline.
