# AI Careers and Compensation Knowledge

## Core Knowledge Summary

AI roles attract among the highest total compensation packages in technology. Packages typically combine base salary, annual bonus, and equity. Equity frequently doubles or triples total pay at frontier laboratories and major technology firms.

### Principal Roles and Ranges (U.S. Averages)

- **AI Research Scientist**: Base $175,000–$350,000+; Total $300,000–$800,000+. Focuses on novel architectures, foundational models, and core theory. Elite frontier-lab packages commonly exceed $600,000–$1,000,000+.
- **AI Solutions / Systems Architect**: Base $145,000–$225,000; Total $200,000–$350,000+. Designs enterprise multi-agent systems and aligns technical architecture with business operations.
- **ML / LLM Engineer**: Base $140,000–$200,000; Total $180,000–$300,000. Builds, fine-tunes, trains, and deploys production machine-learning systems and large language models.
- **MLOps / Infrastructure Engineer**: Base $145,000–$190,000; Total $185,000–$275,000. Owns continuous deployment, GPU/data pipelines, monitoring, and runtime optimization.
- **AI Product Manager**: Base $130,000–$185,000; Total $170,000–$250,000. Translates technical capability into product roadmaps, requirements, and feature delivery.
- **AI Security / Ethics Specialist**: Base $120,000–$180,000; Total $150,000–$230,000. Ensures compliance, safety, robustness, alignment, and protection against adversarial threats.
