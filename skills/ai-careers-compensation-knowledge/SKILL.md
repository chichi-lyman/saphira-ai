## Core Knowledge Summary

AI roles attract among the highest total compensation packages in technology. Packages typically combine base salary, annual bonus, and equity. Equity frequently doubles or triples total pay at frontier laboratories and major technology firms. Compensation intensity rises with the scarcity of expertise required to design, govern, and verify systems advancing along the intelligence continuum.

### Principal Roles and Ranges (U.S. Averages)

- **AI Research Scientist**: Base $175,000–$350,000+; Total $300,000–$800,000+. Focuses on novel architectures, foundational models, and core theory. Elite frontier-lab packages commonly exceed $600,000–$1,000,000+. Aligns most closely with Stage 2–3 research frontiers.
- **AI Solutions / Systems Architect**: Base $145,000–$225,000; Total $200,000–$350,000+. Designs enterprise multi-agent systems and aligns technical architecture with business operations. Bridges Stage 1 production systems with Stage 2 multi-agent generality.
- **ML / LLM Engineer**: Base $140,000–$200,000; Total $180,000–$300,000. Builds, fine-tunes, trains, and deploys production machine-learning systems and large language models. Core Stage 1 excellence contributing to Stage 2 continuous-learning capabilities.
- **MLOps / Infrastructure Engineer**: Base $145,000–$190,000; Total $185,000–$275,000. Owns continuous deployment, GPU/data pipelines, monitoring, and runtime optimization. Ensures reliable Stage 1 substrate and observability for safe Stage 2–3 advancement.
- **AI Product Manager**: Base $130,000–$185,000; Total $170,000–$250,000. Translates technical capability into product roadmaps, requirements, and feature delivery. Converts Stage 1–2 capability growth into user-facing value under governance.
- **AI Security / Ethics Specialist**: Base $120,000–$180,000; Total $150,000–$230,000. Ensures compliance, safety, robustness, alignment, and protection against adversarial threats. Essential across all stages and increasingly central as systems approach Stage 3 recursive improvement and Stage 4 acceleration.

## Alignment Principle

The escalating complexity of each stage in the intelligence continuum corresponds to rising demand for specialized human talent and correspondingly higher total compensation packages. Roles operating closer to frontier research, systems architecture, and safety/alignment work command the highest packages.
