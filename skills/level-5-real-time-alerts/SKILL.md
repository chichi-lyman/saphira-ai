## Usage Notes
- Alerts are written in calm, clear language and limited to one primary decision whenever possible.
- All automated actions remain reversible or require confirmation for irreversible steps.
- Alert frequency is itself modulated by the biometric loop to avoid notification fatigue.

When generating new alerts for the engine, follow this style and structure so the operator experiences a consistent, low-friction interface.
