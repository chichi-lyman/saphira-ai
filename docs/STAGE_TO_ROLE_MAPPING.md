## Detailed Role Alignment

### Stage 1 – Artificial Narrow Intelligence
- **ML / LLM Engineer** — production training, fine-tuning and deployment of domain-specific models.
- **MLOps / Infrastructure Engineer** — pipelines, monitoring, GPU scaling and reliability.
- **AI Product Manager** — translating specialized capability into user-facing value.
- Supporting classical software, data engineering and domain specialists.

### Stage 2 – Artificial General Intelligence
- **AI Solutions / Systems Architect** — multi-agent workflows, cross-domain integration and governed autonomy.
- **AI Research Scientist (applied)** — continuous learning, transfer and world-model construction.
- **Quantum Applications / Quantum ML Scientist** — hybrid classical-quantum algorithms that expand problem classes.
- Expanded Product and Architecture roles that orchestrate multi-domain strategies under policy.

### Stage 3 – Artificial Superintelligence
- **AI Research Scientist (frontier)** — novel architectures, self-improvement proposals and scientific discovery pipelines.
- **AI Security / Ethics Specialist** — alignment, robustness, adversarial defense and verification of recursive systems.
- **Quantum Algorithm Researcher** — algorithms that may accelerate scientific search and optimization at super-human scale.
- Multi-agent swarm orchestration and large-scale verification talent.

### Stage 4 – Technological Singularity
- Intensified demand for **AI Security / Ethics**, governance, audit, simulation and multi-party approval mechanisms.
- Policy, standards and international coordination roles that maintain inspectability and rollback capacity.
- Human (or multi-stakeholder) oversight remains the primary safeguard while acceleration outpaces individual monitoring.

### Stage 5 – Post-Human & Transhuman Intelligence
- Neurotechnology, BCI hardware/software, hybrid identity/memory researchers and ethicists.
- Consent frameworks, cognitive continuity contracts and safety specialists for direct neural interfaces.
- Cross-disciplinary talent spanning neuroscience, AI and ethics.

### Stage 6 – Universal (Cosmic) Intelligence
- Long-horizon systems architects, physics-informed designers and value-alignment stewards operating at planetary or larger scales.
- Governance principles (inspectability, auditability, alignment) remain normative invariants even at cosmic substrate.

## Ethics Frameworks Across Stages

OECD, UNESCO, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, IEEE Ethically Aligned Design and the EU AI Act provide the normative and operational scaffolding that must scale with capability:
- Soft-law principles (OECD, UNESCO) set the value baseline at every stage.
- Operational risk management (NIST) and certifiable management systems (ISO 42001) become increasingly critical from Stage 2 onward.
- Binding regulation and rigorous verification intensify as systems approach Stage 3–4 recursive improvement and acceleration.
- Human oversight and multi-stakeholder governance remain non-negotiable until Stage 5 hybrid regimes potentially redefine the boundary.
