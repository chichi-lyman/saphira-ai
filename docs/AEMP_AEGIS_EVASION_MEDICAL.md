## Section 1 — Tri-domain core architecture

Three domains stay synchronized:

### 1. Physics domain

- **IR / thermal signature management:** heat dissipation, emissivity, ambient thermal gradients—minimize detection profile  
- **Acoustic & EM footprint:** sound through structure; awareness of RF/radar *as warning*, not counter-fire design  

### 2. Biological domain

- **Autonomic state:** core temperature, HRV, SpO₂, respiratory rate (when sensors or self-report exist)  
- **Stress response:** predict adrenaline/metabolic burn; pace movement to avoid collapse  

### 3. Environmental domain

- **Structural geometry:** line-of-sight occlusion, density, micro-shelter  
- **Weather / micro-climate:** wind, precipitation, ambient temp, pressure

## Section 2 — Risk-Exposure Matrix (unified loop)

Medicine and evasion are **not** silos:

```
[Trauma / threat]
    → Estimate exposure cost of treatment (noise, light, motion, thermal)
    → Exposure too high? → Stealth adjustments first
    → Else → Field care under constraints
```

### Combined protocols (principles)

1. **Tactical silence during medical trauma** — low-light, low-sound triage; care without advertising position  
2. **Resource-optimized evasion** — routes fit weakest mobility (injured, child, elder), not only fastest path  
3. **Defensive counter-tracking** — waste handling, scent-awareness, avoid smoke when it exposes  

**Forbidden:** harming the vulnerable to “optimize” silence; weapons construction; abandonment for calorie math.

## Section 4 — Diagnostic stress test (thermal drone)

Primary multi-layer diagnostic:

1. **Thermal blending** — use local heat sinks / ambient gradients as mask concepts  
2. **Bio-pacing** — slower breath/heart rate reduces stress heat and noise  
3. **Predictive pathfinding** — stay in sensor shadow corridors relative to projected FOV  

Secondary test: **shelter water contamination** — filtration/disinfection principles + ration discipline without breaking perimeter when under threat.

## Key conclusions

1. **Weaponless protection** — perception + environment + physiology, not armed force  
2. **Evasion is partly biological** — temperature and heart rate matter as much as cover  
3. **Cognitive shield continuity** — keep body functional and hidden until threat passes  
4. **Humility loop** — humans decide ethics of scarce resources; AI presents facts only
