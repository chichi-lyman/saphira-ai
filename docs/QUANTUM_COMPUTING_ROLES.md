## Market Context

The pure-play quantum workforce remains relatively small (approximately 16,500 professionals globally in 2025) while demand continues to outpace supply. Compensation is competitive with senior software and research roles in classical AI, reflecting specialized physics, mathematics and engineering expertise. Total compensation frequently includes base salary, annual bonus and equity, especially at startups and large technology laboratories.

Median U.S. quantum computing salaries cluster in the $110k–$160k range across roles, with senior research and staff-level positions substantially higher.

## Principal Roles and Approximate U.S. Compensation

### Quantum Algorithm Researcher / Quantum Research Scientist
- **Typical Range:** $150,000 – $250,000+ base; total compensation frequently $220,000 – $400,000+ at senior/staff levels (top laboratories may exceed $500k–$600k total).
- **Focus:** Design of novel quantum algorithms, theoretical research, circuit optimization, resource estimation and collaboration with hardware teams.
- **Education:** Usually PhD in Physics, Computer Science, Mathematics or related field.

### Quantum Hardware Engineer
- **Typical Range:** $140,000 – $220,000+ base; total compensation $200,000 – $360,000+ at senior levels.
- **Focus:** Design, fabrication, cryogenics, control electronics, characterization and scaling of physical qubit platforms (superconducting, trapped-ion, neutral-atom, photonic, etc.).
- **Education:** Master’s or PhD in Physics, Electrical Engineering or related discipline.

### Quantum Software Engineer / Quantum Software Developer
- **Typical Range:** $130,000 – $200,000+ base; total compensation $170,000 – $320,000+ at senior/staff levels.
- **Focus:** Compilers, SDKs, simulators, error-mitigation software, hybrid classical-quantum workflows and production tooling (Python, C++, Rust common).
- **Education:** Master’s or strong Bachelor’s in CS/Physics plus quantum software experience.

### Quantum Error Correction (QEC) Researcher / Engineer
- **Typical Range:** $145,000 – $240,000+ base; total compensation comparable to algorithm research roles at senior levels.
- **Focus:** Fault-tolerant architectures, logical qubit design, decoder algorithms and surface-code or alternative QEC schemes.

### Quantum Applications / Quantum ML Scientist
- **Typical Range:** $140,000 – $210,000+ base; total compensation $190,000 – $320,000+.
- **Focus:** Mapping real-world problems (chemistry, optimization, machine learning, materials) onto quantum or hybrid algorithms; resource estimation and application benchmarking.

### Post-Quantum Cryptography Specialist
- **Typical Range:** $130,000 – $200,000+.
- **Focus:** Design and migration of cryptographic systems resistant to quantum attacks; standards work (NIST PQC).

### Quantum Product / Program / Sales Engineer Roles
- **Typical Range:** $120,000 – $230,000+ (sales/BD roles may include significant commission).
- **Focus:** Translating technical capability into product roadmaps, customer solutions or commercial engagement.

## Notes

- Ranges are approximate market averages derived from 2025–2026 job postings, industry reports and salary databases; actual offers vary by location (U.S. coasts higher), company type (startup equity vs. large-lab stability), and individual impact.
- Hardware and algorithm research roles generally command premiums relative to pure software roles at equivalent seniority because of scarcity of experimental and theoretical expertise.
- Quantum talent frequently intersects with classical AI/ML skill sets (hybrid algorithms, quantum machine learning), creating natural career bridges.
