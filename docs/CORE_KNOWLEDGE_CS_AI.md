# Saphira Core Knowledge — CS & AI Program Structures

Canonical reference for advanced domain understanding of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence undergraduate structures at top-20 institutions. This knowledge is injected into Saphira’s semantic memory and capability routing to support precise technical reasoning, curriculum-aware guidance, and interdisciplinary synthesis.

## 1. Standalone Undergraduate Degree Majors

- Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (B.S. C.S.) offered across all top-20 institutions.
- Bachelor of Science in Artificial Intelligence (B.S. A.I.) (e.g., Carnegie Mellon University).
- B.S. in Artificial Intelligence and Decision Making (e.g., MIT — Course 6-4).
- B.S. in Computer Engineering (C.E.) (e.g., Stanford, Columbia, Northwestern).
- B.S. in Data Science & Machine Learning (e.g., UC Berkeley, Carnegie Mellon, NYU).

## 2. AI Specializations & Concentration Tracks (Within CS)

At institutions such as Stanford, Harvard, and Princeton, students earn a CS degree while specializing in an official track:

- **Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning**: Deep learning, reinforcement learning, probabilistic modeling, and knowledge representation.
- **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**: Computational linguistics, speech recognition, large language models (LLMs).
- **Computer Vision & Visual Computing**: Image processing, 3D graphics, generative visual models, and AR/VR.
- **Robotics & Autonomous Decision Systems**: Intelligent kinematics, mechatronics, motion planning, and autonomous navigation.
- **Human-AI Interaction (HAI)**: User-centered AI design, trustworthy AI systems, and ethics in automated systems.

## 3. Interdisciplinary Joint Majors (STEM + CS/AI)

- **Computation and Cognition**: CS combined with cognitive science and brain research (e.g., MIT Course 6-9, Yale).
- **Computational Biology / Bioinformatics**: CS combined with molecular genetics and algorithms (e.g., Stanford, MIT, CMU).
- **CS & Applied Mathematics / Statistics**: Algorithmic optimization, quantitative modeling, and data analytics.
- **Electrical Engineering & Computer Science (EECS)**: Hardware-software integration, system design, and AI compute architectures.
