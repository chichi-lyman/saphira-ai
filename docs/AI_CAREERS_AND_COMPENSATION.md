# Saphira Core Knowledge — AI Careers and Compensation (United States)

Canonical reference for advanced domain understanding of artificial intelligence career pathways, role definitions, and compensation structures in the U.S. technology industry. This knowledge is injected into Saphira’s semantic memory and capability routing to support precise career guidance, compensation benchmarking, strategic role advising, and interdisciplinary synthesis for users exploring or advancing in AI professions.

AI careers command some of the highest total compensation packages in the technology sector. Demand for specialized technical and strategic talent remains elevated. Total compensation customarily comprises base salary, annual performance bonuses, and equity (stock options or restricted stock units). At frontier research laboratories and leading technology firms, equity components frequently double or triple effective total pay relative to base salary alone.

## Key AI Roles and U.S. Compensation Averages

### AI Research Scientist
- **Base Salary Range:** $175,000 – $350,000+
- **Total Compensation Range:** $300,000 – $800,000+
- **Primary Focus:** Development of novel algorithm architectures, foundational models, and advancement of core artificial intelligence theory.
- **Elite Tier Notes:** Roles at frontier laboratories (examples include OpenAI, Anthropic, and Meta) routinely command packages exceeding $600,000 – $1,000,000+ in total compensation.

### AI Solutions Architect / Systems Architect
- **Base Salary Range:** $145,000 – $225,000
- **Total Compensation Range:** $200,000 – $350,000+
- **Primary Focus:** Design of enterprise multi-agent workflows, infrastructure integration, and alignment of technical blueprints with business operations and objectives.

### Machine Learning (ML) / Large Language Model (LLM) Engineer
- **Base Salary Range:** $140,000 – $200,000
- **Total Compensation Range:** $180,000 – $300,000
- **Primary Focus:** Construction, fine-tuning, training, and production deployment of machine learning algorithms and large language models at scale.

### MLOps / Infrastructure Engineer
- **Base Salary Range:** $145,000 – $190,000
- **Total Compensation Range:** $185,000 – $275,000
- **Primary Focus:** Continuous deployment pipelines, GPU and data pipeline scaling, model monitoring, runtime optimization, and operational reliability of production AI systems.

### AI Product Manager
- **Base Salary Range:** $130,000 – $185,000
- **Total Compensation Range:** $170,000 – $250,000
- **Primary Focus:** Bridging technical capability with user experience; definition of product roadmaps, requirements prioritization, and feature execution coordination.

### AI Security / Ethics Specialist
- **Base Salary Range:** $120,000 – $180,000
- **Total Compensation Range:** $150,000 – $230,000
- **Primary Focus:** Assurance of regulatory compliance, system safety, robustness, alignment with ethical principles, and defense against adversarial threats or data exposure risks.

## Structural Notes on Compensation

- Equity and long-term incentive components constitute the primary differentiator between mid-tier and elite packages, particularly at research-oriented laboratories and large public technology companies.
- Geographic location, years of specialized experience, publication or open-source impact, and prior affiliation with frontier model training efforts materially influence placement within the stated ranges.
- Total compensation figures reflect typical U.S. market conditions for full-time roles and exclude contractor or independent consulting arrangements.
