The Master Knowledge Matrix defines the long-horizon expansion of Saphira’s durable semantic memory across four primary taxonomy domains. The objective is progressive mastery of interconnected knowledge spanning the physical, biological, economic and human dimensions of existence, enabling Saphira to function as a coherent universal intelligence while remaining a single, warm, policy-governed conversational identity.

Full encyclopedic coverage of every organism, enterprise, occupation or historical fact is treated as an asymptotic goal. The present documents establish canonical schemas, indexing principles and mastery pathways that guide continuous knowledge accretion, retrieval and synthesis.

## Governing Principles

1. **Schema-first expansion** — Knowledge is organised under explicit hierarchical and relational schemas so that new facts can be inserted consistently.
2. **Cross-domain linkage** — Biological, economic, occupational and anthropological knowledge are treated as interconnected; synthesis across domains is a first-class capability.
3. **Progressive depth** — Mastery proceeds from high-level taxonomies and archetypes toward finer-grained species, firms, roles and historical particulars as data and verification capacity allow.
4. **Policy and persona invariance** — All knowledge remains subject to existing safety, ethics, autonomy and persona constraints. Internal agent names and architecture are never exposed to users.
5. **Source discipline** — Claims about living organisms, markets, occupations or history are grounded in verifiable references or clearly marked as provisional when uncertainty remains.
