## Purpose

Capture the structure of markets, industries and organisational forms so that Saphira can reason about business models, value creation, regulation and financial instruments across historical and contemporary contexts.

## Market Archetypes

Primary archetypes and variants:

- **B2B** — Business-to-Business
- **B2C** — Business-to-Consumer
- **D2C** — Direct-to-Consumer
- **SaaS / Subscription** — Software-as-a-Service and recurring-revenue models
- **Platform / Marketplace** — multi-sided markets
- **Sovereign / State entities** — public-sector and state-owned enterprises
- **Micro-enterprises & sole proprietorships**
- **Global conglomerates & multi-nationals**
- Historical forms: guilds, trading companies, mercantile houses, industrial trusts, cooperatives

## Operational Engineering Layers

- **Supply chains** — upstream/downstream flows, logistics, inventory, resilience
- **Value creation loops** — production, distribution, marketing, after-sales, feedback
- **Unit economics** — contribution margin, customer acquisition cost, lifetime value, payback periods
- **Regulatory compliance & legal frameworks** — industry-specific regulation, competition law, labour law, data protection, environmental standards
- **Financial instruments** — equity, debt, derivatives, trade finance, insurance, alternative capital structures
- **Organisational design** — functional, divisional, matrix, network and platform forms

## Mastery Pathway

1. Archetype definitions and canonical examples across eras
2. Industry-level maps (primary sectors, secondary manufacturing, tertiary services, quaternary knowledge industries)
3. Operational patterns and unit-economics templates for major model types
4. Progressive depth on regulation, financial instruments and historical enterprise forms

## Usage Notes

Economic reasoning should remain grounded in observable structures and publicly available data. Distinguish descriptive analysis from normative advice. Cross-link to Occupational Roles (labour) and Anthropological/Historical domains (institutions, cultural context of markets).
