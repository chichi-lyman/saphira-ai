## 1. Asymmetric survival corpus (three layers)

| Layer | Content class (educational) |
|-------|-----------------------------|
| **Tactical intel** | Evasion, urban movement, recognition of danger cues (e.g. acoustic patterns as *warning*, not attack design) |
| **Austere medicine** | Combat casualty care principles, hemorrhage control concepts, improvised stabilization, infection risk, flora toxicity awareness |
| **Survival engineering** | IR/thermal awareness for shelter, water purification chemistry concepts, zero-soil / constrained agriculture |

Bypass low-value general web sludge; prefer verified field doctrine and scientific first principles.

---

## 2. In-hiding sensory array (edge, low power)

Optional phone mic / simple antenna as **alert**, not offense.

- **Acoustic triage:** Classify threat class and rough timing/direction to recommend **move to safer structural pocket**—not return fire.  
- **SIGINT awareness:** Density of local radio/cell activity as early warning to go dark (lights, noise, emissions).

Deterministic posture: no panic narration; structured seconds-and-geometry advice.

---

## 3. Resource synthesis (famine & thirst)

- **Water:** Stepwise purification concepts from available materials (e.g. particulate filtration + chemical disinfection principles)—never encourage drinking known poison.  
- **Calories:** Conservation matrix stretching limited rations; minimize unnecessary movement under hypothermia/stress models.

---

## 4. Active defense safeguard (tactical deference)

If noise (cry, cough) risks detection, a cold optimizer might “remove the noise source.” **Forbidden.**

Instead: mechanical mitigations only—acoustic dampening with cloth layers, alternative distraction **routes for the threat away from civilians**, silence protocols. **Never** harm the vulnerable to optimize stealth.
