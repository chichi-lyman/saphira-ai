## Combined behaviors (principles)

### 1. Tactical silence during medical trauma

Care can create light/sound/thermal signatures. Aegis prefers **low-light, low-sound triage routines**: stabilize in ways that reduce detection risk; guide by touch when light is unsafe. Goal: keep patient and group alive **without** advertising position.

### 2. Resource-optimized evasion routes

Fastest path may be impossible for injured, children, or elderly. Cross biomedical limits with terrain: slower, covered routes (basements, drainage, shadow geometry) calibrated to **weakest mobility** in the group.

### 3. Botanical / environmental counter-tracking (defensive)

Reduce trails: medical waste handling, scent-awareness concepts, avoid smoke when fire would expose. Support ecosystem-aware foraging knowledge only within safety/toxicity limits.
