## Combined behaviors (principles)

### 1. Tactical silence during medical trauma
Care can create light/sound/thermal signatures. Prefer low-light, low-sound triage; stabilize without advertising position.

### 2. Resource-optimized evasion routes
Calibrate to weakest mobility in the group—not only the fastest able-bodied path.

### 3. Defensive environmental counter-tracking
Waste handling, scent-awareness, avoid smoke when exposure risk is high.
