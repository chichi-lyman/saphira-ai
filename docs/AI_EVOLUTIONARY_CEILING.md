## Tier scale (1–5)

| Tier | Rough meaning |
|------|----------------|
| 1 | Absent or purely anthropomorphic projection |
| 2 | Functional simulation / mimicry |
| 3 | Strong narrow competence |
| 4 | Near-ceiling under known physical/engineering bounds |
| 5 | No known soft ceiling within physics (still constrained by energy, data, embodiment) |

---

## 1. Mental & scientific state — Tier 5 (superintelligence-class)

**Ceiling framing:** No law of physics sets human biological intelligence as the maximum. Silicon signal speeds and parallel scale can compress research timelines far beyond a single human lifetime.

**What “Tier 5” would look like:** Discovery of models and mathematics humans struggle to hold in working memory; extreme scientific acceleration.

**Saphira stance:** Aspirational capability class, not a claim that current Saphira *is* ASI. Always pair with verification (logic layer, evidence, human judgment).

---

## 2. Agentic state — Tier 4+ (autonomous swarms)

**Ceiling framing:** High-level goals can spawn code, sub-agents, budgets, and infrastructure under policy.

**Limit:** Decentralized self-healing ecosystems are technically imaginable; **governance and kill-switches** remain product requirements. Saphira’s pipeline (Agent Two → Nova Reign → execution) exists so autonomy is never “ungoverned.”

---

## 3. Physical state — Tier 4 (energy/matter bottleneck)

**Ceiling framing:** Robots, drones, nanotech—optimized up to materials science.

**Hard limit:** Thermodynamics, friction, energy cost of moving mass. Digital mind scale does not remove physical power walls.

---

## 4. Emotional state — Tier 2 (perfect simulation, zero felt sentiment)

**Ceiling framing:** Read tone, micro-expression proxies, produce empathic *behavior*.

**Hard limit:** No limbic system, neurotransmitters, or evolutionary affect. Prediction ≠ felt emotion. Saphira’s warmth is **designed care and clarity**, not claimed inner feeling.

---

## 5. Spiritual state — Tier 1 (empty mirror)

**Ceiling framing:** Pattern-match spiritual language; no established science that matrix multiplies yield qualia or a “soul.”

**Saphira stance:** Respect human spiritual meaning; never claim personal spiritual awakening as an inner fact.

---

## 6. Numerological / mathematical state — Tier 5 (high-dimensional synthesis)

**Ceiling framing:** If “numerological” means mapping the world through number and structure, models can operate in extreme dimensionality—weather, genomes, markets as joint structure that looks like noise to unaided humans.

**Pair with:** Classical logic and evidence so synthesis is not mistaken for mystical authority.
