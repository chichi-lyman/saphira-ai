## 5. Fulfillment SOP for the first 5–10 orders

1. Verify paid status in the payment provider dashboard or verified payment event.
2. Confirm the order references the correct product.
3. Deliver the current approved file using the storefront or a controlled link.
4. Record order ID, payment status, delivery timestamp, and any issue.
5. Send transactional confirmation.
6. Offer an optional feedback request; do not condition delivery on a review.
7. Process refund requests under the published policy.
8. Reconcile captured amount, refunds, fees, chargebacks, and net receipts.

Never fulfill based solely on a screenshot, DM, or success-page redirect.

## 6. Weekly metrics worksheet

Record by post and campaign:
- Date, platform, post ID, hook, CTA.
- Reach and profile visits.
- Unique keyword requests.
- Unique product-page visitors.
- Checkout starts and verified paid orders.
- Gross receipts, refunds, fees, chargebacks, net receipts.
- Delivery failures and support requests.
- Qualified implementation inquiries, proposals, and active recurring contracts.
- Notes and next experiment.

**Important:** one-time vault sales are revenue, not MRR. MRR includes only active recurring contracts, counted according to their actual billing terms.

## 7. Implementation service discovery form

Use this as a conversation guide, not an automatic qualification verdict:
1. What does the business sell and to whom?
2. Which channel receives leads?
3. Roughly how many inbound inquiries arrive each month?
4. What tools are currently used for messaging, CRM, booking, and payments?
5. Which process is most costly or error-prone?
6. What actions must always receive human approval?
7. Who owns the connected accounts and can authorize access?
8. What outcome and acceptance test would define success?

After discovery, send a written scope covering deliverables, exclusions, integrations, fees, recurring support, data handling, timeline, acceptance criteria, and cancellation terms. Never promise a revenue outcome.

## 8. Decision scorecard

**GO** when target buyers purchase, understand the scope, receive the file reliably, and support/refund load is manageable.

**REVISE** when there is a measurable leak:
- requests but no clicks → improve CTA and reply;
- clicks but no checkout → improve page clarity, trust, and offer fit;
- checkout starts but no payment → inspect checkout friction;
- buyers confused → improve scope and onboarding;
- service inquiries not qualified → tighten audience and discovery.

**STOP / reposition** after multiple adequately distributed tests show weak qualified interest and no willingness to pay. Small samples or low reach are inconclusive.
