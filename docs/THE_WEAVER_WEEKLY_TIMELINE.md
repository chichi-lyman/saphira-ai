## Mon–Tue: Choice Mapper (career dilemma)

**Scenario:** Offer = +$22k salary, city COL +20%, commute +45 min/day. Anxiety loops and family debate.

1. **Intention dump** — voice rant: numbers, partner concerns, fear of leaving team, long-term goals.
2. **Structural decomposition** — nodes:
   - Hard metrics (green): salary, tax, rent index
   - Soft friction (orange): growth, partner job market
   - Emotional (blue): routine comfort, relocation stress
3. **Ripple projection** — extra 90 min driving → evening leisure −30% → cooking window loss → takeout +$400/mo → sleep efficiency −12% (pattern-based illustration).
4. **Net reality ledger** — raise may shrink to a much smaller effective monthly gain after COL and commute overhead.

**Outcome:** Clarity without the AI deciding for the user.

---

## Wednesday: Crisis Synthesizer (mid-week emergency)

**Scenario:** Fridge dead (groceries spoiled), check-engine light, $1,200 savings vs ~$1,950 combined quotes.

1. **Multi-modal ingestion** — mechanic printout photo, bank screenshot, appliance estimate; “Fix this chaos.”
2. **Physical vs financial risk grid** — refrigeration = high daily health/food risk; example O2-sensor-class code = possible safe short deferral (illustrative).
3. **30-day path** — prioritize safe food storage; defer non-critical car work if risk model allows; negotiation/warranty script **stubs**.

**Outcome:** Panic buffered; ordered actions instead of payday-loan reflexes.

---

## Thursday: Energy Tracker (burnout wall)

**Scenario:** Report due, dirty apartment, utility bill; ~8% brainpower; frozen at the screen.

1. **Capacity set** — mental battery ~8%.
2. **Load filtering** — hide high-EF work; Momentum Mode.
3. **Micro-step** — e.g. pay the one-tap utility; then “three pieces of trash off the counter.”
4. **Dopamine bridge** — acknowledge completion; next micro-step only.

**Outcome:** Initiation without demanding full executive function.

---

## Fri–Sun: Symptom Detective (weekend fatigue pattern)

**Scenario:** Crushing fatigue Fri–Sun for a month; normal bloodwork; no energy for meticulous logs.

1. **Passive notes** — casual texts when wiped out.
2. **Fusion** — notes + wearable sleep + calendar + optional environment.
3. **Correlation** — illustrative pattern: low Thursday deep-sleep **and** Friday afternoon extra caffeine co-occur with weekend crash.
4. **Micro-experiment** — one change next Friday (e.g. herbal tea instead of late coffee); review next Sunday.

**Outcome:** Hypothesis + experiment—not a diagnosis.

## Unified life system

| Feature | Day-to-day | Life outcome |
|---------|------------|--------------|
| Choice Mapper | Life choices → factual node architectures | Clarity; less regret spiral |
| Crisis Synthesizer | Multi-part emergencies → risk triage | Fewer panic financial mistakes |
| Energy Tracker | Tasks ↔ real-time capacity | Momentum instead of guilt |
| Symptom Detective | Casual notes + passive data | Hidden lifestyle levers |

Together they form one **cognitive-load ecosystem** under Saphira—not four disconnected chatbots.
